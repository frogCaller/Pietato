The sounds in this folder are the Soundboard's starter pack (Apps > Soundboard). They were made for Pietato by the
script beside them, make.py: each one is worked out from sums (sines, noise, envelopes), so nothing in them is anyone
else's recording, sample or tune. Run make.py and the same files come out.

To the extent possible under law, Pietato's authors have waived all copyright and related or neighbouring rights to
these sounds: they are in the public domain under Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal.
    https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Use them for anything, changed or not, without asking and without saying where they came from.

  Starter   Air horn, Rimshot, Sad horn, Ta-da, Applause, Crickets, Ding, Buzzer
  Drums     Kick, Snare, Hi-hat, Open hat, Clap, Tom, Cowbell, Crash
  Arcade    Laser, Pickup, Jump, Power up, Explosion, Boing, Siren, Game over

Each is a 48 kHz, 16-bit, one-channel WAV file named for its sound.

Sounds of your own go in the Soundboard folder of your home folder. Those are yours (or their makers'): nothing here
says anything about them.
